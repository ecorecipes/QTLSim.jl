Naturality and model morphisms

Roadmap items C2 (naturality as the validation harness) and C7 (morphisms between models), with the usability items of F11 that fall out of them (explain, the support matrix). The aim is consolidation: the category theory does work that the package did by hand, one script or one if at a time.

Agreement between backends is a naturality square

The life cycle is one morphism f of a free symmetric monoidal category, and each backend is a functor out of it: the kernel K(f) (a random map of Worlds), SLiM (lower(f, :eidos)), the moments of a Gaussian population M(f), and closed forms of theory. A statistic σ maps the states, or the output tables, of one backend to those of another: a kernel population's sample mean and variance are a moments state; the kernel's records are the columns SLiM prints. The central empirical claims of the package are squares

σ ∘ K(f) = M(f) ∘ σ

that commute exactly (in law: the kernel and SLiM; to rounding: the neutral moments and deme_coalescence) or up to a closure's error (Gaussian selection, the rare-alleles closure). Every script of validate/ checks some such square on some cells.

Cases as data

A square is a NaturalityCase: the life cycle (cycle, a morphism; nothing is QTSim's generation), the parameters (params, one per deme for a metapopulation), the two backends (from, to among :kernel, :slim, :moments, :theory), the statistics (statistic => tolerance pairs of QTLSim.Statistics, each a function of a backend's output table, the same function for the backends that share columns), the criterion, the number of replicates of the stochastic side, the horizon, options for the backends, and the verdict (expected) the ported script recorded on the same cell. The criteria are the scripts' rules, written before the runs:

criterionsidesfails when
:bandstochastic → deterministicthe whole 95% CI of the replicates' mean lies outside prediction·(1 ± tol)
:zstochastic → deterministic (exact predictions)`
:exactdeterministic → deterministicthe largest relative difference (a vector: every generation) exceeds tol
:ksstochastic → stochasticthe two-sample Kolmogorov–Smirnov p-value is below tol

check_naturality runs a case and returns a NaturalityResult (per statistic the two sides with standard errors, the measure, pass/fail, and whether the expected verdict was reproduced). The kernel side records QTSim's columns plus each deme's mean genotype (geno_mean), which the variance among deme means needs, through the image of the record step; the SLiM side runs the generated model (slim_script) and parses its table.

validate/naturality_cases.jl is the table, validate/naturality.jl runs it (writes validate/naturality.log). A new step, model or closure gets its validation by adding a row. The scripts and their logs stay: they are the record, and the rows are independent replications of some of their cells (the harness's seeds are its own).

The square of each step

naturality_steps checks one generation step by step. It starts from matched states: a kernel population w (burned in, then its alleles shuffled among chromosomes locus by locus, so it is in linkage equilibrium and Hardy–Weinberg proportions) and the moments state σ(w) with its mean genotype and genetic variance. It applies the steps of the life cycle in order, to many copies of w with their own random numbers and to σ(w), and compares after each step the observables of the step's codomain stage (mean genotype, genetic variance, number of individuals). The first square that fails, after squares that hold, names the step that breaks naturality.

On 100 loci, N = 1000, VP = 1, h² = 0.5, MutationScalar 0.02, μ = 0.01, burned in for 2000 generations and shuffled to linkage equilibrium, 200 replicates of one generation (`naturalitysteps(p; loci, replicates = 200, burnin = 2000)`); V_G at each stage:

(ω = 4.) The aggregate recursion's square fails first at reproduce: it inherits all of selection's reduction, while recombination and segregation restore about half of it. The multilocus model's squares hold at a 5% tolerance (its reproduce is 3.6% high, the same sign as its equilibrium bias in validate/moments_loci.jl, kernel/prediction 0.96–0.99). Under strong selection (ω = 1, VG/s ≈ 0.7: VG 4.06 → 1.09 among the survivors) select still commutes (−0.2%) but reproduce misses by +9.5% for :multilocus and +9.6% for :rare_alleles: the kernel restores more variance than the Gaussian closures predict. This localisation is new: the scripts compare only whole runs at equilibrium. It is a finding about the closures (one generation from linkage equilibrium), not a change to them.

The lax version and Lean

Lean proves the exact version: stochastic_lowerings_agree (Lowering.lean) says that two lowerings into the Kleisli category of PMF that agree on every step agree on every life cycle, and lower_conj (Plasticity.lean) says the same for lowerings that agree after a map of states κ (a naturality square with σ = κ). The harness tests a lax version: each step's square commutes up to an error εs, measured on the laws of the statistic. If every step's image is Ls-Lipschitz for that measure (a step does not amplify a discrepancy by more than L_s), a composite of n steps commutes up to

ε(f) ≤ Σ_s ε_s · Π_{s' after s} L_{s'},

by telescoping: replace one step's image at a time. For the exact steps (develop, record, rescale, mutate's expectation, swap, neutral migration) εs = 0, which is the Lean case; selection's ε is the closure's error (Gaussian truncation, the rare-alleles closure's fourth cumulant); reproduce's is the LD the closure forgets (zero for the multilocus model from linkage equilibrium, all of it for the aggregate recursion, as the per-step demo shows). What the scripts and the harness measure is ε(f) on whole runs; `naturalitysteps` measures ε_s.

Claims that would need Lean lemmas:

  1. the telescoping bound above, for Kleisli morphisms of PMF with a metric on laws (for example total variation, or a Wasserstein distance on a real statistic), from per-step bounds and Lipschitz constants;
  2. that the kernel's develop, record, rescale and swap images commute exactly with the moments statistic (σ = sample mean and variance), and mutate in expectation (Mutation.lean has the expectation; the square is not stated);
  3. the reductions declared below that Lean does not have: the n = 1 and independent-trait embeddings (two copies of the 1 × 1 recursion), the projection of the records, one deme as a metapopulation (Demes.lean has the products, not p ⊗ demes(1) = World(p)), the haploid reduction of the multilocus model to the aggregate recursion, and the spectrum's limits;
  4. the order of closures as an order: that each arrow's reduction composes (rare local → rare alleles → multilocus → aggregate under the conjunction of their conditions).

Model morphisms and the order of closures

model_morphisms() declares the maps between models, and QTLSim.closure_order() the arrows between the closures of the moments lowering. Each QTLSim.ModelMorphism states what it maps, under which condition its reductions hold, and which Lean theorem (if any) proves them, and lists QTLSim.Reductions on small cases: bitwise where the code computes the same numbers, to a stated tolerance otherwise. A testset iterates over them ("declared model morphisms: generated reduction tests"); the hand tests they repeat (covers) stay.

:auto from the declarations

Each closure (QTLSim.ClosureDecl, QTLSim.closures()) declares its rank in the order, its domain (where run_moments accepts it) and its automatic domain (where loci = :auto may choose it), as predicates on a QTLSim.ClosureContext: the model, the number of demes, the ploidy, whether the mutate steps carry one law of mutation effects, and between. loci = :auto is QTLSim.auto_closure: the finest closure whose automatic domain contains the context.

A test enumerates 72 contexts (four models, both ploidies, 1, 2 or 4 demes, no, equal or different mutation fields on the mutate steps) and checks that the declarative rule gives exactly what the hand-written rule gave; it does in all 72. run_moments resolves :auto without between, as before: with between in the context the declarations would pick :multilocus for haploid metapopulations, which the lowering then rejects anyway (a first version passed between and differed from the old rule in exactly those 18 rejected cases). The one difference is outside the lowering and is kept as found: QTLSim.moments_loci(p) (which multilocus_equilibrium uses) returns :rare_local for a heritable reaction norm, whose moments run_moments runs with :multilocus, as the declarations do. Another test checks the declared domains against run_moments' guards over 240 combinations: they agree but for one gap, also kept as found: several traits in several demes accept loci = :spectrum (the guard is only on the one-deme path) and silently run the rare-alleles closure.

The support matrix and explain

support_matrix probes 20 features × 3 models × 7 columns (the kernel, the generated SLiM model, the moments lowering with each closure) on tiny populations, so a cell is unsupported exactly where the code's own guard throws an ArgumentError; a cell that runs is supported when the declared evidence names a validation script that compares it with another backend, untested otherwise. support_markdown writes the support matrix page (scripts/support_matrix.jl; a test keeps it current). explain answers the same question for one parameter set: the model, each closure's domain and automatic domain with the declarations' reasons, the closure :auto picks, and a probe of each backend.

The first generation of the matrix (54 supported, 223 untested, 134 unsupported, 6 not applicable) found 3 cells that fail with an error other than an ArgumentError (marked !): slim_script for two traits with Plasticity set, which checkparams rejects ("Plasticity with NoTraits > 1 needs ReactionNorm"), fails inside the Eidos splice of the fecundity images ("the Eidos image of select has changed") before any check of the parameters. Kept as found (reported, not fixed).
