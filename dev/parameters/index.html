Parameters

A simulation is described by a Params value. Its defaults are QTSim's (SetDefOptions in reference/QTSim/src/PassOptionFile.c), and checkparams checks the values when a World is made. Copy one with changes with Params(p; field = value). read_qtsim_options and write_qtsim_options translate QTSim's options files.

What the parameters set, and what they don't

PhenotypeVar and Heritability set the environmental variance VE = (1 − h²)·VP, the mutation variance and the analytical width ω. They do not set the realised variances. The genetic variance starts at zero (every allelic effect is 0) and settles where mutation and selection balance. That level depends on m, ω, the number of loci and N, and can be well above or below h²·VP. So the realised VP and h² of a run are outputs, not inputs.

Each generation the optimum θ moves, from MoveFitnessSDGen on, by MoveType:

  • :phenotype: θ = z̄ + √V_P·d, a fixed lead over the current mean phenotype;
  • :fitness: θ = z̄ + ω·d, the same in units of the fitness width;
  • :continuous: θ = θ + d, a constant rate of environmental change; the paper's published runs use this.

Fitness is QTSim's Gaussian pdf(Normal(0, ω), z − θ) / (0.4/ω), which keeps QTSim's 0.4 in place of 1/√(2π), so the fitness at the optimum is 0.997 rather than 1. Selection is viability selection: an individual survives if a uniform draw falls below its fitness. The population dies when fewer than two survive (one, if haploid).

Population size

QTSim keeps the population at PopSize: however few survive selection, they produce PopSize offspring. A population there dies only when fewer than two survive a single generation, and it pays nothing for a low mean fitness until then.

With Demography = :beverton_holt the survivors' offspring are density-regulated. With n survivors, carrying capacity K = PopSize and R = GrowthRate, the next generation has Poisson(R n/(1 + (R − 1) n/K)) individuals, at most K (noffspring). A population with mean fitness W̄ settles near K (R W̄ − 1)/((R − 1) W̄) and shrinks when R W̄ < 1, so it can decline and be rescued by adaptation, or die. The lowering to SLiM covers QTSim's fixed size only.
